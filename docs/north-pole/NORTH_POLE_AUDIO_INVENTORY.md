# North Pole audio inventory

North Pole: The Missing Christmas Star ships with no audio files. Every cue below is **Missing**:
the game plays silence until staff upload audio and publish a sound release (ADR-050). Missing
audio is silent, never an error. Cue definitions live in `lai/apps/audio/north_pole_cues.py`; the
client tables in `react/src/features/region-play/audio/regionAudio.ts` (region id
`north-pole-village`). Never commit audio to the repository.

## Licensing requirement

Every recording must be **original** (made for the project), **public domain**, or **licensed**
for this use, and its licence recorded in `ASSET_LICENCES` and in the asset's licence field
before the asset is published. No audio is downloaded or generated by the build.

Seasonal music needs extra care:

- Do not use a modern recording of a traditional carol unless the recording itself is licensed.
  The tune of an old carol may be public domain while a particular recording, arrangement or
  performance is not.
- Prefer original instrumental pieces (music box, soft bells, celesta, gentle strings) or a
  licensed seasonal library whose licence allows use in a children's app.
- No religious lyrics and no vocals. The celebration is secular (lights, sleigh, friends).
- Child friendly: calm, no sudden loud peaks, nothing frightening. Never a buzzer for a wrong
  answer; the retry cue is soft.

## Suggested sourcing

| Kind | Suggested source |
|---|---|
| Music (village, forest, celebration) | Commissioned or owner-composed instrumental; a licensed royalty-free seasonal library (check commercial and children's app terms); public domain compositions with a self-made performance |
| Ambience beds | Self-recorded field sounds (wind, snow, ice), or a licensed sound library with a commercial licence |
| Footsteps, chimes, fanfares | Self-made foley (snow in a bag of cornflour, glass bell), or a licensed library |
| Reindeer, sleigh bells | Self-recorded real bells; a licensed animal sound library |

## Owner steps in Django Admin

1. Open the Asset Library, choose Add asset. Set kind to Audio, the asset id from the table above,
   category Music for music and voice cues, Sound for ambient and sfx. Record the licence in
   `ASSET_LICENCES` and in the asset's licence field.
2. Upload the file as a new version (OGG preferred, MP3 accepted) and publish that version.
3. Run `python manage.py seed_north_pole_sound` (add `--dry-run` first to see what is missing).
   It creates the cue (shared, with the loop, fade and cooldown settings from the table) and, once
   the `north-pole-village` 3D Area exists with its zones, the ambience and music assignments. It
   never overwrites a cue or assignment staff changed, never makes a placeholder and never
   publishes. Run it again after the Area seed.
4. Alternatively add the cue by hand under Sound cues, pointing at the published asset, and add
   Sound assignments for the 3D Area `north-pole-village` (zone target per zone id, event Enter).
5. Open Sound releases, publish a new release, and check the manifest for the Area. Rollback is
   available from the same screen.
6. Hear it in game: the child needs audio unlocked by a first tap and the Music, Ambient or Sound
   effects volume above zero in the sound panel.

## Call points for the integrator

The checkpoint, respawn, zone ambience, music, collect, answer and quest complete cues are driven
by engine events and moments the play layer already raises. The rest need one call each:

| Moment | Call | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Star fragment collected | `playCue('crystalCollect')` | after a fragment pickup is confirmed |
| Christmas Star relit | `playCue('restoration')` | on the `NORTH_POLE_STAR_RESTORED` confirmation |

Reindeer, sleigh bells, footsteps and the greeting sting have no call point yet. Once each is
called, set `wired=True` for that cue in `north_pole_cues.py` and update the table above.
