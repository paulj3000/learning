# Storykeeper Castle: jumping inventory (SC-JMP)

Status: implemented. Owner rule: jumping is **not** a separate adventure category. Every primary castle adventure has meaningful jumping through the **shared platforms runtime** (`react/src/features/island-map/three/platforms/*`; ADR-055 to ADR-059, `docs/PLATFORMING.md`, `docs/PLAYER_MOVEMENT_AND_JUMPING.md`). There is no new movement engine and no castle-specific physics. Learning stays primary: a child can finish every adventure and show mastery without jumping once.

Preparation audit: `docs/regions/prep/jumping_prep.md`. Engine integration audit (per room, every movement criterion) and migration plan: `docs/regions/storykeeper_castle_engine_integration.md`. Contracts it extends: `docs/regions/storykeeper_castle_contracts.md` (C-2 claims, C-3 HUD equivalence).

| Platforms never grade or reward | A pad has a ground-plane answer zone `pad:*`. Entering it is an ordinary `PlayerEnteredZone`. `usePadAnswers` hands the answer to the session's own `submitAnswer`; the adventure engine grades; the server writes any reward. | Clear of everything | Each pad is inside its room, 0.15 m from every wall, clear of every doorway and its approach, 0.3 m from every safe spot and checkpoint, clear of colliders, props, characters and every zone anything listens to. | `castlePlatforms.test.ts` (geometry block) |

### Deviations and decisions to confirm

1. **Two Observatory stones, not five.** The audit proposed five stones. The Setting Tower is the castle's most crowded room: its arrival zone, three window zones (each a `choose-setting` answer, so a stone must not overlap one), the telescope, the orrery, the door approach and the checkpoint at its centre leave exactly two places where a 0.8 m stone fits (the scan is reproduced by the geometry test). The audit's coordinates also put stones on window zones, which would let a hop choose a setting by accident. Two stones it is. The Observatory has no graded content, so nothing is lost.
2. **The Painted Words moved into the world.** Before, the wall's `START_ADVENTURE` went through the generic panel to the card route. For the pads to answer, `castle-tale` now claims the wall's two interactions for Pathfinders (`paintedWordsAdventureFor`, C-2 kept: a claim is made only for a band the mission is authored for) and hosts the same adventure in the room, exactly as it does the Tale. Other bands are unchanged.
3. **`SIGNAL` for pathway pads.** The audit named `WORLD_STATE`; a `WORLD_STATE` platform reads the world only when the scene is built, so a page restored this visit would not appear until the next one. The shared runtime already turns every recorded change into the signal `world:<changeKey>`, on entry and live (the view does this today), so world-change pages use it; only the discovery page, which has no signal, uses `WORLD_STATE`.
4. **Footprints.** 0.8 m to 0.9 m (the shared runtime's minimum top is 0.8 m), not the audit's 1.2 m: the Studio and the Tower cannot hold 1.2 m pads clear of their live zones.
5. **Pad pictures.** Pads are coloured blocks. The pad guide line names the word on each colour for the card and for a child who cannot see the pad. Drawing the word and picture card on the pad needs a label asset path that does not exist yet (the bank's `pictureId` is carried in `PaintedWordsPadSlot` for it).
7. **GE-072 dropped the first star stone.** With the child's 0.37 m body (ADR-060, from the shared movement stack) the stone at (6.75, 7.45) touched the tower checkpoint and the arrival through `arch-tower`, and a scan of the tower found no other spot clear of the window, chart and setting zones, the checkpoint and the door. The Observatory is flavour only, so nothing is lost. The pad clearance rule now uses the body: 0.45 m from every safe spot, checkpoint and door arrival (`castlePlatforms.test.ts`).
6. **SC-R05-03 moved the second star stone.** Professor Orion stands in the tower's south-east corner, where the second stone was. The stone moved 0.25 m west (x 8.95 to 8.7); it is still 0.1 m clear of the cave window zone and 0.15 m from Orion's collider (`castlePlatforms.test.ts`, `castleSupportingNpcs.test.ts`).

## 4. Owner-approved exceptions
