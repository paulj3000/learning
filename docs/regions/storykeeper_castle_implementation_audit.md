# Storykeeper Castle — Implementation Audit (SC-R01-01)

**Written 2026-11-02 on `adventures/storybook`** (based on `origin/dev20261102`,
`eaea5c5`). This is the baseline for the Storykeeper Castle Reboot
(`docs/regions/STORYKEEPER_CASTLE_REBOOT_ROADMAP.md`). It records what the
repository contains today, measured from code rather than from the earlier
roadmaps' status lines, and classifies each finding as:

- **Working**: implemented and covered by automated tests.
- **Partial**: real code exists, but a reboot requirement is not yet met.
- **Missing**: no code exists (grepped for, not assumed).
- **Browser-only**: implemented, but the only proof that would count is a
  real browser or device run, which has not happened.

Baseline checks run for this audit: 37 castle and asset Vitest files, 504
tests, all passing; `npm run typecheck` passes. No browser was launched.

Paths are relative to `react/src/` unless they start with `lai/`, `docs/`,
`scripts/` or `amplify/`.

## 1. Room layout and connectivity — Working

Source: `features/island-map/three/storykeeperCastleRegion.ts` (pure data, no
`three` import). Ground extent 32 × 20 m, wall height 3 m (`sceneKit.ts`
`WALL_HEIGHT`), walls 0.5 m thick straddling each room edge.

| Room id | Child-facing label | Floor (x, z, metres) | Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `entry-hall` | the entry hall | x −16..−9, z −3..3 | 7 × 6 |
| `story-hall` (hub) | the story hall | x −9..3, z −6..6 | 12 × 12 |
| `character-gallery` | the Character Gallery | x −8..−1, z 6..10 | 7 × 4 |
| `costume-room` | the Costume Room | x −8..−1, z −10..−6 | 7 × 4 |
| `east-corridor` | the east corridor | x 3..5, z −10..10 | 2 × 20 |
| `setting-tower` | the Setting Tower | x 5..10, z 5..10 | 5 × 5 |
| `illustration-studio` | the Illustration Studio | x 5..10, z −1..3 | 5 × 4 |
| `great-library` | the Great Library | x 5..15, z −10..−2 | 10 × 8 |

Seven archways (`ARCHWAYS`) connect a hub-and-spoke plan: the entry hall,
gallery, costume room and corridor open off the story hall, and the tower,
studio and library open off the corridor. The Great Library is the deepest
room. Walls are **derived** (`buildWallSegments`: room floors minus archway
gaps), so a doorway cannot be open on one side and sealed on the other.
Nothing is gated by geometry. `storykeeperCastleRegion.test.ts` flood-fills
every room from spawn.

Five checkpoints (`features/discovery/checkpoints.ts`): `entrance`,
`story-hall`, `gallery`, `tower`, `library`. The spawn is the entrance.

There is no vertical traversal. `firstPersonController.ts` is flat-plane.

## 3. Lighting and materials — Partial

- Engine (`three/runtime/createLocationEngine.ts`): one `AmbientLight`, one
  `DirectionalLight`, and the manifest's `lights` as `PointLight`s, which can
  be gated by world-state requirements.
- Castle: ambient 0.3, sun 0.25, background `0x14171d`. There is one **white**
  point light per room with authored intensities, plus doorway and hearth
  lights. `castle-tale` adds a doorway glow.
- Renderer: `{ antialias: true }` only. No pixel-ratio policy, tone mapping,
  shadows, fog or post-processing.
- Materials: every generated part is a flat `baseColorFactor` with fixed
  metallic 0.1 and roughness 0.85, double-sided (`assets/gltfAssembler.ts`).
  An optional `emissive` exists. There is **no shared runtime material or
  palette module**. The only palette is the generator's `CASTLE` constant.
- There are no quality presets.

`castle-story-hall` and `castle-secret-door` for every band, but
`CastleTaleCompanion` only acts when the band resolves to content. An
Explorer who opens the story hall, or a Pathfinder who opens the secret door,
gets a claimed interaction that starts nothing and is never closed. This is a
bug fix allowed under ADR-020 and is scheduled as SC-R01-04's contract item
C-2.

**Finding AUD-2 (card route).** `routes/IslandLocationPage.tsx` uses the first
template for the location and does not call `resolveAdventureForAgeBand`.
Sprouts and Explorers see "not available for your age yet" on the card
castle. It also links to the Phaser castle and the 3D castle for every band.

## 7. Progress and save/resume — Working on Amplify, Missing on Django

What the castle writes today, all through Amplify Data:

| Record | Writer |
| --- | --- |
| Adventure session and steps | `features/adventures/api.ts` (Amplify) |
| Story progress, one record per story (ADR-027) | `features/story/` (Amplify `ChildStoryProgress`) |
| World changes (`FIRST_STORY_TOLD`, `SECRET_DOOR_CLUES_COLLECTED`, `CASTLE_SECRET_DOOR_OPENED`, `THE_CASTLES_SECRET_DOOR_COMPLETE`) | Amplify |
| Checkpoints, discoveries, met characters | `features/discovery/api.ts` (Amplify) |
| NPC dialogue state | `features/npc/api.ts` (Amplify `ChildNpcState`) |

Resume: a session left open resumes in the room. `CastleTaleCompanion`
re-reads the chosen hero and setting from the recorded answers, so it needs
no new persistence.

Django (`lai/`) has **no castle-specific code** (grep "castle" or
"storykeeper" across `lai/` finds nothing). It has the canonical models the
castle will move onto:

- `progression.StoryProgress`, which has no writer service and no API yet;
- `progression.WorldChange`, append-only and unique per child, location and
  key, which Treasure Rush and quests write but no story code does;
- `progression.WorldState`, which has no writer;
- `inventory.InventoryItem`;
- `progression.NpcRelationship`;
- `catalog.Quest` with `quests.QuestProgress`.

`the-storykeepers-tale` is imported as a catalog `Adventure` on Location
`storykeeper-castle`. The secret-door adventures carry location slug
`castle-secret-passage`, which has no Location, so their FK is null. Story
arcs are **not** imported (no story catalog model).

The castle is a **Place** (`catalog.Location`) but **not a 3D Area**: no
`regions.WorldRegion` is seeded for it. Its layout is the TypeScript
manifest, as ADR-033 requires.

Consequence for the reboot: anything persistent that SC-R04 and SC-R07 add
(pages, artifacts, ranks) must not invent a castle-specific store. Section
4 of the reboot roadmap derives pages from existing world changes and puts
artifacts in the existing inventory catalog.

## 9. Asset-resolver capabilities — Working (bundled), Partial (Django)

- Pipeline: id → `AssetResolver` → url → `GLTFLoader`
  (`three/assets/assetResolver.ts`). `BUNDLED_ASSET_RESOLVER` reads
  `manifest.ts`.
- Django adapter: `api/assets/djangoAssetResolver.ts`. It batches
  `GET children/<id>/assets/?ids=`, falls back to bundled, and parses model,
  image and audio families (ADR-035, ADR-045).
- **The castle never uses the Django resolver.** `regionAssetsFor`
  (`features/region-play/regionAssets.ts`) returns the bundled loader for
  every source-controlled region. Swapping a model is a one-line
  `manifest.ts` url edit with no game-logic change, but it still needs a
  rebuild.
- `mediaAssets.ts` has `loadTexture` and `resolveSound` keyed by logical id.
  Neither is used by any region.
- Missing-model fallback: `createLocationEngine.ts` draws a placeholder box
  (`addMissingModel`) for props, models and NPCs, and a flat plane for tiled
  ground. This is tested in `runtime/missingModelPlaceholders.engine.test.ts`.

## Inventory: what to keep, extend, replace

**Must remain unchanged** (the reboot's foundation):
- Room coordinates, archways and derived walls (`storykeeperCastleRegion.ts`).
- Every interaction id, requirement and action in `worldObjects.ts`.
- `adventureBindings` and every option id. All authored adventure and story
  text.
- ADR-027 single story-progress semantics. The resume-from-answers
  behaviour.
- State-variant pairs (identical vertices) and gameplay-bearing geometry
  (rod lengths, star counts).
- HUD equivalence and the "Things to do" alternative. Sprouts reticle
  suppression.
- The Phaser castle and Writing Room, until SC-R10's replacement passes its
  gates.

**Extend:**
- Generic manifest and engine: material presets, textured surfaces, quality
  tiers, light moods, ambient effects (SC-R02-01, 02-06). This is generic,
  so other regions benefit.
- The generator's material story (ADR-028 option C) and new decorative
  scenery assets (SC-R02).
- Reduced-motion support in the 3D runtime (SC-R02-05).
- The existing `tell-a-story-together` quest pattern, which becomes the
  model for the Great Storybook quest (SC-R04, a `quests.json` entry rather
  than a new backend).
- Inventory items and collectible sets for artifacts (SC-R07).
- The NPC framework for Ember and supporting NPCs (SC-R05).

**Replace:**
- Flat untextured scenery surfaces: floors, walls and ceilings move to
  textured surfaces (SC-R02).
- White per-room lights move to room mood presets (SC-R02-06).
- The Phaser Writing Room moves to a Three.js room (SC-R10).

**Blocked on something outside this branch:**
- Sound (Sound Manager merge, ADR-047).
- Polly and voice consent (decision pending).
- A castle `WorldRegion` and Django story progress (LAI-0309, LAI-0603,
  LAI-0709).
- Commissioned character and landmark art (budget decision, ADR-028).
- Real-device profiling and the Sprouts playtest (owner).
