# Firepeak audio inventory

The Heart of Firepeak ships with no audio files. Every cue below is **Missing**: the game plays
silence until staff upload audio and publish a sound release (ADR-050). Cue definitions live in
`lai/apps/audio/firepeak_cues.py`; the client tables in
`react/src/features/region-play/audio/regionAudio.ts`. Never commit audio to the repository.

## Licensing requirement

Every file is owner-supplied (recorded under their own licence or created for the project) and its
licence must be recorded in `ASSET_LICENCES` before the asset is published. No audio is downloaded
or generated by the build. Child-friendly: calm, no sudden loud peaks, nothing frightening.

## Owner steps in Django Admin

1. Open the Asset Library, choose Add asset. Set kind to Audio, the asset id from the table above,
   category Music for music and voice cues, Sound for ambient and sfx. Record the licence in
   `ASSET_LICENCES` and in the asset's licence field.
2. Upload the file as a new version (OGG preferred, MP3 accepted) and publish that version.
3. Run `python manage.py seed_firepeak_sound` (add `--dry-run` first to see what is missing). It
   creates the cue (shared, with the loop, fade and cooldown settings from the table) and, once
   the `firepeak-heart` 3D Area exists with its zones, the ambience and music assignments. It
   never overwrites a cue or assignment staff changed. Run it again after the Area seed.
4. Alternatively add the cue by hand under Sound cues, pointing at the published asset, and add
   Sound assignments for the 3D Area `firepeak-heart` (zone target per zone id, event Enter).
5. Open Sound releases, publish a new release, and check the manifest for the Area. Rollback is
   available from the same screen.
6. Hear it in game: the child needs audio unlocked by a first tap and the Music, Ambient or Sound
   effects volume above zero in the sound panel.

## Call points for the integrator

Checked on `main` `f0de1087` (2026-11-24). `crystalCollect` (`RegionPlayCompanion`) and
`switchFlip` (`AreaPuzzleLayer`) are called. **Defect:** `puzzleComplete` and `restoration` are
mapped in `regionAudio.ts` but nothing calls them, so `sfx.firepeak.puzzle_complete` and
`sfx.firepeak.restore` stay silent even with audio uploaded, although `firepeak_cues.py` marks
them `wired=True`. `sfx.firepeak.machinery` and the optional greeting are unwired by design.
Where each moment should be called:

| Moment | Call | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ember shard collected | `playCue('crystalCollect')` | after the pickup is confirmed |
| Puzzle solved | `playCue('puzzleComplete')` | when the server confirms completion (AreaPuzzleLayer) |
| Heart restored | `playCue('restoration')` | on the restoration COLLECT_OBJECT confirmation |
| Switch flipped | `playCue('switchFlip')` | forge switch interaction |

Once each is called, set `wired=True` for that cue in `firepeak_cues.py`.
